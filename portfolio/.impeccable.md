## Design Context

### Users
Product design hiring managers and recruiters — primarily at FinTech and tech companies — scanning multiple portfolios quickly. Desktop-first context. They need to be convinced fast, then pulled deeper into the work. The job to be done: find signal that this designer thinks carefully, ships real work, and has a distinct point of view.

### Brand Personality
Voice: direct, precise, with personal warmth earned through details rather than stated. Not warm-and-fuzzy; warmth comes through the cat, the garden, the poetry interaction — all discovered, never announced.

3 words: **Thoughtful. Curated. Professional.**

Emotional goal for a visitor: *clean + confident* — "This is professional and well-crafted."

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: Light mode only. White (#FFFFFF) surface, near-black (#1A1A1A) ink, red-orange (#EC4523) accent. No dark mode planned.
- **Layout**: 12-column grid, 24px mobile gutters, 72px desktop gutters. Whitespace is structural — density signals content weight, not effort.
- **Typography**: Geist Sans (geometric, 400/500) is the workhorse. Tiempos Text (serif) reserved for authentic personal register moments (e.g., About heading). Font shifts signal a change in register, not decoration.
- **Interaction details**: Custom triangle cursor, dotted underline links that turn accent-red on hover, superscript number badges on external links, active-page triangle indicator in nav — craft in details, never announced.
- **Motion**: Snappy and spring-based (230–520ms, cubic-bezier ease-out). Animations clarify state or ease transitions; they don't perform. `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected.
- **Personal layers**: Fufu the cat, interactive pixel garden, draggable polaroids, poetry interaction. These appear as discovered easter eggs, not foreground features.

**Anti-references**: Over-animated, dark/moody, corporate/safe, generic Notion-export template.

### Design Principles

1. **Craft in the details** — The custom cursor, dotted underlines, superscript badges, and triangle nav indicator signal design thinking without announcing it. Every micro-interaction is considered. Add detail at the interaction layer, not the layout layer.

2. **White space is voice** — The layout breathes. Generous padding and a strict grid communicate editorial confidence. Never fill space to fill space.

3. **Personality earns its place** — Personal elements appear as layers to be discovered, not foregrounded. They coexist with professionalism; they never displace it.

4. **Motion serves the read** — Animations clarify transitions, confirm state changes, and ease attention — nothing more. If removing an animation doesn't lose meaning, remove it.

5. **Typography carries register** — Geist Sans handles information; Tiempos Text handles voice. Use serif deliberately and sparingly as a signal that something is personal, not informational.
